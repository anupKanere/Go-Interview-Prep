## 🎯 Interview Preparation

### 📝 Study Schedule
- **Weekdays**: 2-3 hours (1 hour theory + 1-2 hours coding)
- **Weekends**: 4-5 hours (2 hours theory + 2-3 hours coding)
- **Daily**: 3-5 problems (mix of easy, medium, hard)

### 🎯 Problem Distribution
- **Easy**: 40% (build confidence)
- **Medium**: 45% (core interview problems)
- **Hard**: 15% (advanced topics)

### 📊 Progress Tracking
- Keep a log of solved problems
- Note time taken for each problem
- Record common mistakes and learnings
- Track improvement in problem-solving speed

## 🎯 Success Metrics

### 📈 Goals
- Solve 300+ problems total
- Average 20-30 minutes per medium problem
- Can explain solutions clearly
- Comfortable with system design basics

### 🏆 Milestones
- **Month 1**: Master basic data structures

## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Consistency is key**: Practice daily
2. **Quality over quantity**: Understand solutions deeply
3. **Time yourself**: Practice under time pressure
4. **Explain out loud**: Practice verbal explanations
5. **Learn from mistakes**: Review failed attempts
6. **Stay updated**: Follow industry trends
7. **Network**: Connect with other developers
